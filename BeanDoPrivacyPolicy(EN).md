# BeanDo Privacy Policy

## Introduction

BeanDo (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our") takes user privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information, and the rights you have. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our privacy practices.

This Privacy Policy applies to users globally. We will provide privacy protection that complies with local laws according to the legal requirements of different regions.

## Data Storage and Privacy

### 1. Data Storage
- **Local Storage**: All your reminders, tags, and personalized settings are stored locally on your device, managed using Core Data technology.
- **iCloud Backup**: If you choose to enable iCloud backup, your data will be backed up to your iCloud account for backup purposes only.
- **Developer Access Restriction**: We cannot access any of your data, including reminder content, tag information, or personalized settings.

### 2. Data Collection Statement
- **No Collection of Personal Data**: We do not actively collect, store, or analyze any of your personal data.
- **No Device Information Collection**: We do not actively collect your device model, operating system version, or device identifiers.
- **No Usage Data Statistics**: We do not actively collect your app usage frequency or feature usage statistics.
- **No Crash Log Uploads**: Due to the author's emphasis on user privacy, no crash feedback function is implemented within the software. Therefore, we do not automatically upload crash logs or other diagnostic information.

## Use of Information

We use your data only locally on your device for the following purposes:

1. **Providing Core Features**:
   - Storing and managing your reminders.
   - Sending reminder notifications.
2. **Personalized Service**:
   - Saving your settings to provide a consistent user experience.
   - Displaying the app interface according to your preferences.

## Sharing of Information

We do not share your information with third parties for the following reasons:

1. **No Data Collection**: We do not collect any user data that could be shared.
2. **Local Processing**: All data processing is completed on your device.
3. **iCloud Privacy**: If you use iCloud backup, data is only backed up to your iCloud account, following Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy.

We will not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, nor will we use your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Security

We have implemented the following security measures to protect your data:

1. **Data Encryption**: Data is encrypted during transmission and storage.
2. **Backup Mechanism**: When you enable the corresponding backup function, user data is backed up regularly to prevent data loss.

## Permission Usage

### 1. Notification Permission
- **Purpose**: To send reminder notifications, ensuring you receive important reminders promptly.
- **Usage Scenario**: Sending push notifications when a reminder is due.
- **Control Method**: You can turn off notification permission at any time in your device settings.

### 2. Calendar Permission
- **Purpose**: To check if the Chinese regional calendar is used within the calendar app for the implementation of certain specific features.
- **Usage Scenario**: When creating or updating a reminder with a "workday" repeat option, the app automatically identifies whether the Chinese regional calendar is used to determine holiday and make-up workday information.
- **Control Method**: You can turn off calendar permission at any time in your device settings.

### 3. iCloud Backup Permission
- **Purpose**: To back up your reminder data and some app settings.
- **Usage Scenario**: Automatically backing up to iCloud.
- **Control Method**: You can turn off the iCloud backup function at any time in the app settings.

### 4. Microphone Permission
- **Purpose**: For the voice input feature, allowing you to quickly create reminders by voice.
- **Usage Scenario**: Recording your voice when you use the voice input feature.
- **Control Method**: You can turn off microphone permission at any time in your device settings.

### 5. Speech Recognition Permission
- **Purpose**: To convert your speech into text for creating reminders.
- **Usage Scenario**: Recognizing your speech content when you use the voice input feature.
- **Control Method**: You can turn off speech recognition permission at any time in your device settings.
