Last Updated: 2026

Introduction
BeanDo (referred to as "we", "us", or "the Developer") takes your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information, as well as the rights you have. Please read it carefully.

This Privacy Policy applies to users worldwide. We provide privacy protections that comply with applicable local laws in different regions.

1. Data Storage and Privacy
1.1 Data Storage
Local Storage: All your reminders, tags, and personalized settings are stored locally on your device, managed using Apple's Core Data technology.

iCloud Backup: If you choose to enable iCloud backup, your data will be backed up to your personal iCloud account. This feature is used solely for data backup and cross-device synchronization.

Developer Access Restriction: We cannot access any of your data, including reminder content, tag information, and personalized settings. All data exists only on your device and in your personal iCloud account.

1.2 Data Collection Statement
No Personal Data Collection: We do not actively collect, store, or analyze any of your personal data.

No Device Information Collection: We do not collect your device model, operating system version, or device identifiers.

No Usage Statistics Collection: We do not collect your app usage frequency or feature usage patterns.

No Crash Log Uploads: Out of respect for user privacy, we have not integrated any crash reporting framework in the app, so we do not automatically upload any crash logs or diagnostic information.

2. Use of Information
We only use your data locally on your device for the following purposes:

Providing Core Functions

Storing and managing your reminders

Sending local notifications when your reminders are due

Personalization

Saving your settings to provide a consistent experience

Displaying the app interface according to your preferences

3. Sharing of Information
We do not share your information with any third parties, because:

No Data Collection: We do not collect any user data that could be shared.

Local Processing: All data processing occurs locally on your device.

iCloud Privacy: If you use iCloud backup, your data is backed up only to your personal iCloud account, and is subject to Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party, nor do we use your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Security
We protect your data through the following measures:

Local Data Security: All data is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS system security mechanisms.

iCloud Transmission and Storage Encryption: When you enable iCloud backup, data is encrypted by Apple during transmission and storage. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Backup Mechanism: When you enable iCloud backup, your data is automatically backed up to your personal iCloud account to prevent data loss.

7. Permissions
7.1 Notifications
Purpose: To send reminder notifications, ensuring you receive important reminders on time.

Usage Scenario: When a reminder you set becomes due, a local push notification is sent.

Control: You can turn off notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

7.2 Calendar
Purpose: To identify Chinese public holidays and compensatory workdays, used for the "Weekdays only" reminder feature.

Usage Scenario: When you create a repeating reminder and select "Weekdays only", the app reads holiday information from the system calendar to correctly skip public holidays and include compensatory workdays.

Control: You can turn off calendar permissions at any time in your device's Settings. If disabled, the "Weekdays only" feature will not accurately recognize holidays.

7.3 iCloud Backup
Purpose: To back up your reminder data and app settings, enabling cross-device synchronization and data recovery.

Usage Scenario: When you use BeanDo on multiple Apple devices, data is automatically synced via iCloud.

Control: You can turn off iCloud backup at any time in the app's settings.

7.4 Microphone
Purpose: To enable voice input, allowing you to quickly create reminders by speaking.

Usage Scenario: When you use the voice input feature, your speech is temporarily recorded for recognition.

Control: You can turn off microphone permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Data Note: Voice data is used only for the current recognition and is not stored or uploaded.

7.5 Speech Recognition
Purpose: To convert your speech into text for creating reminders.

Usage Scenario: When you use the voice input feature, your speech is recognized and transcribed into text.

Control: You can turn off speech recognition permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Technical Note: This feature uses Apple's Speech Recognition API (SFSpeechRecognizer). All speech processing is performed by iOS either on-device or via Apple's secure servers. We do not upload, store, or access your raw voice data.
